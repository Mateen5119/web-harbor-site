### 1. Introduction
Web Harbor Solutions ("we," "our," or "us") operates the website `webharborsolutions.com` and provides web design, hosting management, and maintenance services[cite: 1]. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website or engage our services.

### 2. Information We Collect
- **Information You Voluntarily Provide:** When you submit a demo request, contact form, or sign up for services, we collect your name, business name, physical business address, email address, and phone number[cite: 1].
- **Publicly Available Information:** In accordance with standard business research, we may collect publicly available business data (such as Google Maps listings, public reviews, and commercial phone numbers) to prepare website prototypes[cite: 1].
- **Automated Technical Data:** When you visit our website, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) automatically logs standard, anonymized network information such as IP addresses, browser types, and access timestamps for security and performance purposes.

### 3. How We Use Your Information
- To create, test, and deliver custom website prototypes[cite: 1].
- To communicate regarding project updates, billing, and technical maintenance[cite: 1].
- To comply with legal obligations, including tax, corporate entity filings (US LLC / SECP Pakistan), and anti-fraud regulations.
- We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

### 4. CAN-SPAM & Outreach Compliance
All commercial email communications sent by Web Harbor Solutions contain clear sender identification, our contact email, and an immediate opt-out mechanism ("Reply STOP to unsubscribe")[cite: 1]. We honor all opt-out requests within 24 hours[cite: 1].

### 5. Data Security
We host all client data on encrypted, enterprise-grade cloud providers (Cloudflare, Google Workspace). However, no transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
