Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, straight answers to the questions local business owners ask most.

What if I don't like the demo website you build?

You pay absolutely nothing. We build the complete demo on our own staging servers before asking for a dime. If it doesn't meet your standards, we shake hands and part ways with zero obligation.

Why is there a 6-month commitment?

Because we invest our own time and engineering capital upfront to build your entire website for free, the 6-month term allows us to recover our build costs while keeping your monthly retainer at an accessible $99/mo flat.

Do I own my domain name?

Yes, 100%. If you already own your domain, you retain full ownership. If we register it on your behalf, we transfer it to your registrar at zero cost upon cancellation after your 6-month term.

What counts as a "minor monthly edit"?

Minor edits include changing phone numbers, swapping photos, updating team bios, modifying operating hours, adding a new seasonal special, or altering service prices. Simply email us and we handle it within 24 hours.
